## Before submitting
- Week 7: connect a projector, calibrate your actual surfaces, save the mapping JSON, and add at least two real installation photos plus your group reflection to `week7/documentation-template.md`.
- Week 8: test each key and click interaction; write your own concept and design reflection.
- Week 9: allow microphone access and test in a quiet/loud environment; document your audio source, data mappings and citations.
