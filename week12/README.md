This is an extension of the Week 11 data-visualization assignment, as specified in the course agenda.

## Project: A Year of Reported City Needs

The sketch queries the NYC 311 public dataset and groups 2025 service requests by month and borough. It adds a time-based view to the Week 11 comparison of complaint categories.

- Select a borough using the on-screen tabs or keys 1–5.
- Press A to compare all boroughs.
- Press R to refresh the API data.

## Research question
How does the volume of NYC 311 service requests change through 2025, and do the five boroughs show similar or different monthly patterns?

## Data source
[NYC Open Data — 311 Service Requests from 2020 to Present](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/erm2-nwe9.json). The sketch filters records to 2025 and groups them by month and borough.

A 311 record represents a request reported through the city's system, not every issue that exists in a neighborhood. Changes in reporting access, awareness, and category definitions can affect the totals.
