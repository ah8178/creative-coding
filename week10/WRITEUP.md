# Week 10 — Gestural Interaction

## Project description
For this project, I explored how hand gestures can become an alternative way to interact with a digital sketch. I used ml5.js HandPose to track the position of my index finger and thumb through a webcam. The index finger controls the position of a circle on the canvas, while the distance between the index finger and thumb changes the circle's size. I used a short easing effect so that the circle follows the hand smoothly instead of jumping between positions. This experiment helped me think about the body as an input device and about how familiar movements can become digital controls.

## Reflection
One thing I found interesting about gesture-based interaction is how technology can interpret movements that we usually make without thinking. It makes me question how much of our body language can be translated into digital information. Facial recognition and body tracking also raise questions about privacy and consent because cameras collect information about people. I found the discussion around bias in facial recognition particularly interesting because technology does not always interpret every individual equally. This made me think about how creative coding can be used not only to create interactive experiences but also to question the technology behind them. I would like to explore how gestures can make digital experiences feel more natural while still giving users control over their personal information.
